Why Normalization is Required

Raw futures quotes cannot be directly subtracted: GOLDM trades in units of ₹ per 10 grams with a 100g contract multiplier, whereas GOLDPETAL trades in ₹ per 1 gram with a single-gram multiplier. Direct difference yields an erroneous basis artifact of ₹64,328.00. The analytical engine transforms all GOLDPETAL quotes via the mathematical operator: P_normalized = P_raw × 10 aligning identical physical bullion weights and exposing the true economic spread of +₹40.00 / 10g.
