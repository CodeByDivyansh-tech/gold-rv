⚠️ The Problem
Contracts differ in lot size (1g to 100g), quote unit, metallurgical purity (995 vs 999) and expiry calendar. A fair comparison must handle carry, thin days, settlement vs real fills, and costs; backtests can cheat with look-ahead bias.
💥 The Impact
Apparent "arbitrage" vanishes after brokerage, taxes (CTT, GST, stamp duty) and slippage. Furthermore, a single historical crash episode can make an otherwise unprofitable rule look deceptively brilliant.
🎯 The Theme
Covers all 5 directions of the hackathon brief using public MCX Bhavcopy only, strictly tracked by expiry date. Under the brief's scientific guidelines, a rigorous "no edge after costs" is a valid and robust outcome.
Methodology & Framework

The Solution

Hover Cards to Flip
1. Relative Value
Priced per gram of pure gold. Flags unusually cheap or rich contracts.
Hover to flip ↻
Every contract normalized mathematically to pure gold equivalent. Normalization formula: close / quote_grams / purity. Eliminates superficial lot and purity distortions.
2. Curve & Carry
GOLDPETAL curve carry (median 8.0% a year). Moves decomposed daily.
Hover to flip ↻
Each day's basis move is split into underlying gold price movement, roll-down (~4 bps) and real curve change (~24 bps). Matches financing and vaulting costs.
3. Walk-Forward Test
Day-by-day replay, past data only, next-day fills, full MCX cost structure.
Hover to flip ↻
Frozen parameters on 24-month Train (2023-2025) and tested on 12 months unseen Test (2025-2026). Decides at close t, fills at close t+1. Stress-tested at 0-10 bps slippage.
4. Quiet Alerts
Five checks including gap >= 2x trading cost. All 6 pairs quiet on 30-Sep-2026.
Hover to flip ↻
Signals require statistical divergence AND edge > 2x round-trip cost (48.8 bps). On 30-Sep-2026, all pairs stay quiet to avoid losing money to transaction friction.
5. Attribution
Profit split into spread, gold, and costs. Gold explains under 1%.
Hover to flip ↻
Net P&L is mathematically reconciled: Spread Gain + Gold Gain - All Costs. Zero beta and near-zero correlation (<0.02) to the gold price confirms true market neutrality.
6. Contract Lifecycle
Calendar of 138 contracts. No entry in last 7 days; forced exit at 4 days.
Hover to flip ↻
Tracks 138 contract lifecycles individually. Prohibits new entries in the final 7 trading days and mandates forced liquidation 4 days before expiry to avoid physical tender.

Core Mathematical Formulation
# 1. Pure Gold Normalization
p = close / grams / purity
# 2. Rolling Out-of-Sample Z-Score
z = (s - mean[past]) / std[past]
# 3. Execution (Zero Look-Ahead)
decide at close t -> fill at close t+1
# 4. Hurdle Check
entry allowed iff |basis_bps| >= 2 * cost_bps
