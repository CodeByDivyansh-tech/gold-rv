⚠️ The Problem

Contracts differ in lot size (1g to 100g), quote unit, metallurgical purity (995 vs 999) and expiry calendar. A fair comparison must handle carry, thin days, settlement vs real fills, and costs; backtests can cheat with look-ahead bias.

💥 The Impact

Apparent "arbitrage" vanishes after brokerage, taxes (CTT, GST, stamp duty) and slippage. Furthermore, a single historical crash episode can make an otherwise unprofitable rule look deceptively brilliant.

🎯 The Theme

Covers all 5 directions of the hackathon brief using public MCX Bhavcopy only, strictly tracked by expiry date. Under the brief's scientific guidelines, a rigorous "no edge after costs" is a valid and robust outcome.

Methodology & Framework

The Solution

1. Relative Value

Priced per gram of pure gold. Normalization formula: close / quote_grams / purity. Eliminates superficial lot and purity distortions.

2. Curve & Carry

GOLDPETAL curve carry (median 8.0% a year). Daily basis move split into underlying gold price, roll-down (~4 bps) and real curve change (~24 bps).

3. Walk-Forward Test

Frozen parameters on 24-month Train (2023-2025) and tested on 12 months unseen Test (2025-2026). Decides at close t, fills at close t+1.

4. Quiet Alerts

Signals require statistical divergence AND edge > 2x round-trip cost (48.8 bps). On 30-Sep-2026, all 6 pairs stay quiet to avoid losing money to friction.

5. Attribution

Net P&L reconciled: Spread Gain + Gold Gain - All Costs. Gold price moves explain under 1% of the profit; correlation of trade returns with gold is -0.10.

6. Contract Lifecycle

Tracks 138 contract lifecycles. Prohibits new entries in final 7 trading days; mandates forced liquidation 4 days before expiry to avoid physical tender.
